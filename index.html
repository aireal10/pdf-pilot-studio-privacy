PDF Pilot Studio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

PDF Pilot Studio works entirely on your computer. Your documents are never uploaded, and the application collects nothing about you.

What the application does with your files

  • Files you open or convert are read from, and the results written to, your own disk. Nothing is sent to any server.
  • The application contains no network features: no account, no sign-in, no update check, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting. Its web view is also configured so it cannot contact outside servers, and the bundled LibreOffice engine runs with its network and external-link features switched off.
  • Your original files are never changed, with one exception that you start yourself: Save in the PDF editor replaces the original after asking you, and by default keeps a copy named name (original).pdf beside it.

What the application stores on your computer

WhatWhereContains
Settings and preferences%APPDATA%\UniversalFileConverterPro and %APPDATA%\PDF Pilot StudioLanguage, theme, defaults you chose, window size and position
Conversion history%APPDATA%\UniversalFileConverterProFile names, sizes, timings and results only — never document content

Passwords you type to open or protect a PDF are used only for that operation and are not written to disk. You can clear the history in Settings, and uninstalling removes the program; your settings and history stay until you delete the folders above.

Microsoft Store

If you obtained the application from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft processes your purchase and licence under its own privacy statement. We receive no personal information from the Store beyond what Microsoft provides in the Partner Center (for example, anonymous download and rating counts).

Children

The application collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
